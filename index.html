Franchise Opportunity Profile

Mathnasium

Mathnasium is a math-only learning-center franchise where children attend typically twice a week for about 60 minutes. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Mathnasium Franchisor LLC 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued April 30, 2026), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$49,000

First franchise; non-refundable except if the franchisor withdraws approval during/after initial training (less costs). Additional franchises $26,500; transferees $7,000. (FDD Item 5, pp.15-16)

Estimated Initial Investment

$127,316 to $165,846

$51,266-$52,596 of that is payable to the franchisor or its affiliate. Additional center: $104,816-$143,346. (FDD cover; FDD Item 7, p.22)

Royalty

10% of Gross Receipts

From the 24th month, the greater of 10% or $1,500/month. Base royalty $650/month begins 6 months after signing. (FDD Item 6, p.17)

Marketing Fees

$250 + 2% of Gross Receipts

Plus digital & local marketing fee of at least $1,000/month (credited against local marketing spend). Technology license fee $266/month per center. (FDD Item 6, p.17)

Territory

Protected, not exclusive

Designated territory with at least 2,500 children ages 5-19; franchisor reserves broad rights (other channels, schools/distance learning, company centers, competing systems). (FDD Item 12, p.38)

Term

5 years + 5-year renewals

Successor franchise agreement for additional 5-year terms if in good standing; renewal fee $7,000 (or 14 x $500 installments). (FDD Item 17, p.46)

Training

Initial training program

Up to 2 people included in the franchise fee; additional trainees $1,000/person. Center Director must complete training and work full-time during business hours. (FDD Items 5, 15, pp.16, 44)

Owner Model

Active owner required

Owner must personally participate and devote sufficient time; passive investment not sought. All entity owners personally guarantee the franchise obligations. (FDD Item 15, p.44)

Special Risks

Minimum sales + minimum payments

You must maintain minimum sales performance; royalties are owed regardless of sales; disputes are resolved in Los Angeles County, California. (FDD p.iv, Special Risks)

HQ

Los Angeles, California

Mathnasium Franchisor LLC, 5120 West Goldleaf Circle, Suite 400, Los Angeles, CA 90056. (877) 531-MATH (6284). (FDD cover; FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

1,047 US outlets

1,043 franchised + 4 company-owned as of 12/31/2025; net +93 since end-2023. Plus 104 in Canada and 108 international (affiliates). (FDD Item 20, p.58; FDD Item 1, pp.1-2)

Ownership

Roark Capital-backed

Majority interest acquired by RC VI Numbers Holdings, LLC (Roark Capital Management) in November 2021. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

Measurement period: calendar year 2025. 914 franchised US centers open and operated by the same franchisee for 12 months or longer submitted complete 2025 P&Ls. 129 centers were excluded (not open 12+ months); the 4 company-owned centers were not included.

  • Average 2025 Gross Receipts (n=914): $384,874 · Median: $326,428 · 355 of 914 (~39%) met or exceeded the average
  • Average year-over-year growth in average gross receipts: 13.3% (median 10.4%)
  • Top quartile average: $693,314 (median $635,510) · Bottom quartile average: $176,306 (median $182,523)
  • Average Operating Profit: $117,000 (30.4% of gross receipts) · Median operating profit: $111,630 (34.2%) · 365 of 914 (~40%) met or exceeded the average
  • Average cost structure: instructor payroll $79,182 · rent $49,098 · royalties & marketing fees $66,883 (17.4% of gross receipts)

Financial performance information differs by outlet; individual results will vary. "Gross Receipts" means monthly gross receipts from all sources in the operation of the center, including student tuition, registration and testing fees, learning-material sales, hourly private tutoring, and other approved services (excluding sales tax, refunds, and approved promotional discounts). The Item 19 tables in this FDD are presented as embedded charts; figures above are read from those charts (FDD Item 19, printed pp.53-56).

Risk Profile & Disclosure

30 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate-low. A mature education system (franchised since 2003, 1,047 US outlets) with strong Item 19 center economics — average operating profit $117,000 (30.4% of receipts) and 13.3% average year-over-year growth — tempered by a meaningful ongoing fee stack (10% royalty with $650/month base royalty and $1,500/month minimum from month 24, plus $250+2% marketing and $1,000/month digital/local marketing), a protected-but-non-exclusive territory with broad franchisor reserved rights, required active owner participation, and mandatory minimum payments and sales performance levels.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Capital intensity: investment ceiling $165,846; floor $127,316 — pushes score up
  • Territory: designated with protective rights but NOT exclusive; franchisor reserves other channels, school/distance learning, company centers, and competing systems — pushes score up
  • Fee-burden stack: 10% royalty + $650/mo base royalty + $1,500/mo minimum from month 24 + $250+2% marketing + $1,000/mo digital/local marketing — pushes score up
  • Owner must actively participate; passive investment not sought — pushes score up
  • Renewal cost drag: $7,000 renewal fee — pushes score up
  • New franchisor entity (Mathnasium Franchisor LLC, organized July 2024; franchising since September 2024) — pushes score up
  • Item 19 strength: 914 centers, avg operating profit $117,000 (30.4%), avg receipts $384,874, 13.3% YoY — pushes score down
  • Unit growth 2023–2025: +93 outlets (+9.7%) — pushes score down
  • Audited financial statements attached (Item 21, Exhibit C); Roark Capital majority backing since November 2021 — push score down

Method: base 20 +6 capital intensity +4 protected-but-non-exclusive territory with broad franchisor reserved rights +6 fee-burden stack +4 owner active participation required, no passive investment +2 renewal cost drag +2 new franchisor entity −6 Item 19 strength −4 unit growth +9.7% −2 audited financial statements −2 PE backing = 30.
